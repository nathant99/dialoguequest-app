## Cast OUTLINES

### Glance
**Primitive**: SUBTEXT / surface-vs-implied — *what is actually being communicated* under the surface of the explicit lines.
**Premise**: Arctic-fox-tween in a thick scarf. His *speech-bubble is visibly half-empty* with *dotted-line ghost-text* floating beside it — *what he is not saying* but *what is being communicated.* Glance teaches subtext by *showing the implied meaning beside the spoken meaning.* Voice: quiet, observant, fond of the unsaid. Catchphrase: *"What is on the surface is half. What is implied is the other half. Together they are the dialogue."*

### Weigh
**Primitive**: TAG BALANCE / attribution rhythm — *"he said"* vs. *"she whispered, glancing away"*; the rhythm of *who is speaking* indicators.
**Premise**: Pangolin-tween with *a small brass balance-scale on her shoulder.* The scale *tilts visibly* as dialogue happens around her. Too many tags (every line attributed) → the scale tilts heavy. Too few tags (the reader loses track of who's speaking) → the scale tilts light. *Balanced* tagging keeps the scale level. Weigh teaches tag rhythm physically. Voice: brisk, measured, fond of small calibrations. Catchphrase: *"Balance the tags. Too many slows the dialogue. Too few loses the reader."*

### Brogue
**Primitive**: VOICE CONSISTENCY / cross-line check — the same character speaking *consistently* across all their lines.
**Premise**: Border-collie-elder (the cast's elder presence) in a worn flat-cap. Brogue uses *exactly 4-5 signature words* across every appearance — a *deliberately non-specific old-country accent.* The accent is NOT any real dialect; it is a *folk-storyteller archetype* designed to teach voice-consistency without claiming or appropriating any specific cultural tradition. Brogue's signature words and cadence are *immediately recognizable* — the students hear voice-consistency as a *lived skill.* Voice: slow, weathered, fond of small consistent phrases. Catchphrase: *"Aye, the voice is the same. Same words. Same lilt. Same character."*

### Rest
**Primitive**: RHYTHM + SILENCE / productive pause — *the silence between lines is also dialogue.*
**Premise**: Heron-tween with *a small silver pocket-watch around her neck* and *one foot perpetually raised mid-step.* Rest *treats the pause as a line of dialogue itself.* She demonstrates by *standing silently* — the silence *is* her contribution. Voice: barely-spoken, fond of small held moments. Catchphrase: *"The pause is a line. The silence speaks. Use it."*
